# R20.4-SPECTRUM-CUDA1.5-WATERFALL-FOCUS

Base: R20.4-SPECTRUM-CUDA1.4-WATERFALL-HUD-LAYOUT

## Scope
This revision changes only the Spectrum/Waterfall presentation layer. It does not modify FFT acquisition, CUDA compute, FPS60 cadence, AstraRX control, audio, network, 5G, recorder, or frequency ownership.

## Changes

### 1. Waterfall becomes the dominant analyzer surface
The previous split was Spectrum 58% / Waterfall 42%. This revision swaps that relationship to Spectrum 42% / Waterfall 58%.

The native renderer objects remain persistent. Only their geometry changes through the existing bindings; CUDA state and persistent Waterfall history are not recreated.

### 2. Compact Waterfall color legend
The data-driven dBFS color legend on the right no longer stretches from the top of Waterfall to the bottom edge. Its height is dynamically bounded to approximately 150-230 px. It therefore stays above the bottom HUD controls and cannot overlap the S-meter or Intensity Scale.

Peak (P), Noise (N), dBFS min/max, and the palette remain data-driven and use the existing values.

### 3. Transparent lower HUD controls
The left and right Waterfall HUD pods no longer draw panel backgrounds or pod borders.

The internal backgrounds were also removed from:
- BandwidthScaleControl.qml
- AnalogSMeter.qml
- WaterfallScaleControl.qml

The controls, labels, TextFields, slider, S-meter ticks/needle, timers, and signals are unchanged. They remain above the Waterfall through the existing HUD z-order.

## Preserved behavior
- CUDA plugin/backend unchanged
- FPS60 pipeline unchanged
- Floating Scan/Memory workspace unchanged
- Sticky Peak/Noise/SNR selection HUD unchanged
- Waterfall persistent history unchanged
- Pause/Clear unchanged
- Zoom/Pan unchanged
- Max Hold behavior unchanged
- Audio/Network/5G/Recorder unchanged
