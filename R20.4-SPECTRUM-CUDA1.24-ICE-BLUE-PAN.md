# R20.4-SPECTRUM-CUDA1.24-ICE-BLUE-PAN

## Scope

CUDA1.24 is a visual-only refinement of the zoom PAN navigator from CUDA1.23.
The operator-selected **Ice Blue** palette replaces the magenta/purple PAN palette.
No pan, zoom, divider, FFT, Waterfall, RF calibration, Intensity Scale, Scan/Memory, or CUDA behavior is changed.

## Runtime file changed

- `SpectrumGLPlot.qml`

## Selected Ice Blue palette

| Element | Color |
|---|---|
| PAN panel | `#13262E` |
| PAN border | `#72B4C6` |
| PAN track | `#315E6B` |
| PAN thumb | `#8ED5E0` |
| PAN text | `#E6FAFF` |

The amber Spectrum/Waterfall divider remains unchanged so the two controls keep distinct meanings:

- Amber = protected vertical Spectrum/Waterfall resize control.
- Ice Blue = horizontal zoom/pan navigation control.

## Interaction behavior preserved

- Divider hold time remains **3000 ms**.
- Divider remains one-shot locked after release.
- PAN is visible only while zoomed (`zoomFactor > 1.0001`).
- PAN remains near the Spectrum divider.
- PAN idle opacity remains `0.28`.
- PAN hover/drag opacity remains `0.96`.
- Opacity transition remains `140 ms`.
- PAN geometry and drag math are unchanged.
- Waterfall history is not cleared by pan/zoom.

## Startup marker

```text
[R20.4-SPECTRUM-CUDA1.24-ICE-BLUE-PAN] dividerHoldMs=3000 dividerPalette=amber panPalette=ice-blue panIdleOpacity=0.28 panHoverOpacity=0.96 zoomPanNearDivider=1
```

## Build impact

`SpectrumGLPlot.qml` is packaged through the application's QRC, therefore rebuild the main `iScanMR10` application.
The CUDA plugin does **not** need to be rebuilt.

## Target validation

On Jetson/Qt 5.15.2 verify:

1. Zoom in until the PAN navigator becomes visible.
2. With the pointer away from PAN, confirm the control is subtle at idle opacity.
3. Hover anywhere over PAN and confirm the Ice Blue control becomes clearly visible.
4. Drag the thumb left/right and confirm existing horizontal pan behavior is unchanged.
5. Confirm the divider is still amber and still requires a 3-second hold before vertical resize.
6. Confirm Waterfall history, Intensity Scale, Pause/Clear, Scan/Memory and frequency tuning retain their existing behavior.
