# R20.4-SPECTRUM-CUDA1.22-HOLD-TO-RESIZE-LOCAL-ZOOM-PAN

## Scope

This revision is derived surgically from CUDA1.21. Runtime changes are limited to `SpectrumGLPlot.qml`.

## Goals

1. Prevent accidental Spectrum/Waterfall resizing.
2. Require a continuous 5 second hold before the divider can be dragged.
3. Re-lock the divider after each completed/cancelled gesture.
4. Move the horizontal zoom-pan navigator from the application bottom edge to the Spectrum/Waterfall divider area.
5. Preserve CUDA/FPS60, FFT, Waterfall history, RF measurement/calibration, Intensity Scale, Scan/Memory, and Pause/Clear behavior.

## Divider safety state machine

```text
LOCKED
  |
  | pointer press
  v
HOLDING (5 s)
  |  - progress bar / countdown shown
  |  - no resize is permitted
  |  - >24 px movement cancels the hold
  |
  | 5 s continuous hold
  v
UNLOCKED / DRAG
  |  - pill displays DRAG
  |  - vertical pointer movement updates split ratio
  |
  | release/cancel
  v
LOCKED
```

The unlock applies to one pointer gesture only. A second resize requires another 5 second hold.

## Divider UX

- Hit target: 300 x 66 px.
- Visible pill: 126 x 36 px.
- Normal label: `HOLD 5s`.
- During hold: `HOLD Ns` plus a local progress bar.
- After unlock: `DRAG`.
- Pre-unlock movement tolerance: 24 px to avoid touch jitter while rejecting obvious swipe gestures.
- Holding alone does not resize. A post-unlock vertical movement is required.

## Zoom pan relocation

The zoom navigator no longer uses `anchors.bottom: parent.bottom`.

It is now centered just below the Spectrum/Waterfall boundary:

```text
Spectrum
------------------ [ HOLD 5s / DRAG ] ------------------
                         [ PAN ======== ]
Waterfall
```

The navigator width is adaptive:

```text
max(320 px, min(760 px, root.width * 0.48))
```

The viewport thumb width is now calculated from the local navigator track width rather than from `root.width`, which is required after relocating the navigator into a shorter local control.

## Preserved behavior

- Spectrum default/min/max split ratios remain 0.42 / 0.25 / 0.72.
- Divider resize updates remain coalesced by the existing 16 ms timer.
- Intensity Scale remains manual/locked as in CUDA1.20/CUDA1.21.
- Pause remains 96 x 40 px.
- Clear remains 88 x 40 px.
- Waterfall history is not cleared by resize or zoom-pan.
- CUDA plugin/kernel/worker are unchanged.
- C++ sources are unchanged.
- Existing tuning and zoom buttons are unchanged.

## Target validation

On Jetson/Qt 5.15.2 verify:

1. Short click on divider does not resize.
2. Immediate drag on divider does not resize.
3. Hold 4 seconds then release does not resize.
4. Hold 5 seconds: label changes to `DRAG`.
5. After unlock, drag vertically and confirm Spectrum/Waterfall split changes.
6. Release and confirm divider locks again.
7. Move more than 24 px during the 5 second hold and confirm arming cancels.
8. Zoom in and confirm the horizontal PAN navigator appears near the divider, not at the application bottom.
9. Drag PAN thumb left/right and confirm viewport movement is unchanged functionally.
10. Verify Pause/Clear, Intensity Scale, tuning, Waterfall persistence, Scan/Memory and bottom application menu still operate normally.
