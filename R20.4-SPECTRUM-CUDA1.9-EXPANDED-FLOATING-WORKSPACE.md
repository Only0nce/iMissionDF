# R20.4-SPECTRUM-CUDA1.9 — Expanded Floating Workspace

## Scope

This revision continues from CUDA1.8 and changes only the Scan/Memory floating-workspace presentation and the internal Scan/Memory viewport geometry needed to prevent clipping.

Runtime files changed:

- `SpectrumGLPlot.qml`
- `LogDeviceScanner.qml`
- `NewMemoryAddEdit.qml`

No C++, CUDA, FFT, audio, network, 5G, WebSocket, frequency-control, or waterfall-history code is changed.

## Root cause

The floating panel was limited to roughly 76% width and 39% height. More importantly, `LogDeviceScanner` consumed nearly the full parent width for the detail grid and then placed its 140 px Back rail to the left of that grid. With only ~24 px of left margin, the Back rail obtained a negative x coordinate and was clipped by the floating panel. The always-on vertical scrollbar could also occupy the same horizontal area as the right-side Scan/Memory/Delete/Filter controls.

## Changes

- Floating workspace target size increased to about 86% of the receiver width and 56% of its height, bounded by the right utility rail, bottom HUD dock, and a top safe area.
- `LogDeviceScanner` main grid explicitly reserves a 172 px right action rail.
- `LogDeviceScanner` detail grid reserves 164 px on the left for the Back rail and 172 px on the right for actions.
- `NewMemoryAddEdit` grid reserves the same 172 px right action rail.
- Floating workspace content receives explicit 10 px left/right and bottom safe margins.

## Preserved behavior

- Floating workspace remains non-modal.
- Spectrum/Waterfall geometry and CUDA/FPS60 renderer remain persistent.
- Opening/closing Scan/Memory does not recreate the analyzer.
- Waterfall history is not cleared.
- Right Waterfall color rail and Scan/Memory launcher remain visible.
- Bottom HUD cards remain visible.
- Existing Folder -> Result/Channel -> Detail, Delete and Filter logic is reused unchanged.

## Expected result

- `< Back` is fully visible and clickable in Scan group detail view.
- Grid scrollbars do not sit under the action rail.
- More folder/channel content is visible inside the floating workspace.
- No content is clipped at the floating panel edges under the target 16:9 layouts.
